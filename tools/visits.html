Estate operations

Site visits & issue management

Record inspections, triage resident reports, log corrective actions and evidence, and escalate to letters when needed. Everything here forms part of the estate’s records.

Management access

This console writes to the estate’s official records. Enter the management key (held by the managing agent’s office).

Working practice

Triage new reports on the Issues tab — acknowledge, set severity and a target date, then log every corrective action with evidence photos. Anything that is a bylaw breach rather than a maintenance fault should be escalated with the letters tool, which records issuance and appeal rights. Site inspections are recorded on the Site visits tab; any finding can be raised into a tracked issue with one tick.
