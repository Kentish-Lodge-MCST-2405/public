> Please do **not** post completed forms or personal documents publicly (e.g. GitHub Issues). Submit them directly to the Management Office.

## 1. Why you must register

All vehicles entering Kentish Lodge must be registered with the Management before an RFID tag for the barrier/gantry system can be issued. This is required under the Car Park Label Bylaws (in particular Rule 3 — Proof of Ownership) and the Parking Bylaws. Vehicles without a valid registration and RFID tag may be denied entry or clamped/towed under the Enforcement and Power to Detain and Remove Vehicles bylaws.

## 2. Supporting documents you MUST submit

Submit **ONE** of the following for each vehicle, whichever applies:

| Your situation | Document to submit |
| --- | --- |
| Vehicle is registered in your name | Copy of the vehicle **log card** (LTA Vehicle Registration Card) showing your name |
| Vehicle is rented or leased | Copy of the valid **rental / lease agreement** showing the lessee's name, the vehicle registration number, and the agreement period |
| Vehicle was recently purchased (log card not yet updated) | Copy of the **invoice / sales & purchase agreement** showing the buyer's name and vehicle details |

**If your name does NOT appear on the log card, rental/lease agreement, or invoice:**

You must ALSO submit an **official approval from the person named on the document** — a completed **Letter of Authorisation (Form VR-02)** signed by the registered owner / lessee / purchaser, together with:

- A copy of the authorised signatory's NRIC/FIN (front only); and
- The authorised signatory's contact number (for Management verification calls).

Applications without the required documents will be rejected. The Management reserves the right to verify documents with LTA and to request originals for inspection.

## 7. Part E — Declaration & consent

1. The information and documents provided in this application are true and complete, and I shall notify the Management of any change (change of vehicle, sale of unit, termination of tenancy, or termination of the vehicle lease) within **7 days**.
2. The RFID tag issued is for the registered vehicle only, is **non-transferable**, and must be surrendered to the Management when the vehicle is no longer parked in the estate, upon sale of the unit, or upon termination of the tenancy, in line with the Car Park Label Bylaws.
