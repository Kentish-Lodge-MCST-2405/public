## 1. Purpose

Rules 5 and 6 prohibit noise and offensive conduct, require quiet use of common areas between 10.00pm and 7.00am, and require radios, hi-fi, televisions, instruments and similar equipment to be kept at reasonable volume at all times. This policy sets out how noise and nuisance complaints are recorded, verified and resolved.

## 2. Reporting

Residents may report nuisance through the issue reporter (`/tools/issues.html`) or by calling security when the nuisance is ongoing. A good report states: what was heard/observed, where (unit or area), when it started, whether it is recurring, and any pattern (days/times). Reports are confidential to the management office; the reporter's identity is not disclosed to the other party.

## 3. Response to an ongoing nuisance

1. Security attends, verifies and logs the observation with time and particulars (visit finding).
2. If verified, security gives a verbal request to stop/reduce immediately.
3. The event is recorded against the unit — even if stopped — so patterns are visible later.

## 4. Recurring nuisance (office-led)

1. Two or more verified events in 30 days move the matter to the office.
2. The office issues a first written warning (*warn-1* / *rect-14*), setting out the verified events by date and time and what must change.
3. Continued nuisance draws a final warning (*warn-2*) and may then draw an administrative charge, per the [Compliance & Enforcement Policy](/policies/compliance-enforcement-policy/). Where a nuisance persists beyond the estate's powers (e.g. statutory noise), the office may escalate to the authorities and records that it did.

## 5. Standards for officers

- Verify first: a warning letter states only events the estate verified or can substantiate.
- Late-night call-outs are answered and logged the same night.
- Anonymous reports are actioned for observation, but warning letters are based on the estate's own verified observations.

## 6. Appeals

Warnings and charges under this policy carry the standard 14-day written appeal clause — see the [Appeals Policy](/policies/appeals-policy/).
