## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how the Management Corporation responds when a bylaw is breached — proportionately, consistently, and with a written record at every step. It governs the process only; the bylaws themselves (which cannot be changed by this policy) provide the authority to act.

## 2. Authority

- By-laws of MCST Plan No. 2405, in particular the General Rules Bylaws (Rules 1–3), the Enforcement Bylaws (Rules 21–24, vehicles) and the specific by-law breached in each case.
- This policy does not create, widen or replace any by-law. Where a by-law specifies a charge or procedure (e.g. clamp release charges under Rule 22), that by-law prevails.

## 3. The enforcement ladder

Not every breach needs a letter. Officers apply the lowest rung that resolves the matter, and escalate only when it fails. Every rung from 2 onwards is recorded.

| Rung | Response | Recorded as |
|------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Verbal advice / reminder (e.g. by security or estate staff) | Site visit finding (optional issue) |
| 2 | First written warning — minor breach, no fee; states required action and date | Letter KL-LTR (template *warn-1*) + issue record |
| 3 | Final written warning — breach continues or repeats within 12 months | Letter (template *warn-2*) |
| 4 | Administrative charge, after unanswered final warning or for a serious breach | Letter (template *fee-notice*) |
| 5 | Works action — the MCST remedies the breach at the owner's cost under the by-laws | Corrective action entry + cost recovery file |

Serious breaches (structural danger, fire safety, works without approval) may start at rung 4 or 5 directly. Unauthorised erections, installations and renovation works follow the dedicated 14-day compliance notices (templates *erect-14*, *renov-14*, *rect-14*) rather than the warning ladder.

## 4. Procedure

1. **Observe and record.** Breaches come to light through resident reports (issue register), site visits (visit findings), or staff observation. The recording officer states facts only: what, where, when, weather/conditions where relevant, with photos where possible.
2. **Verify.** Check the issue register and letters register for prior breaches by the same unit in the last 12 months to determine the correct rung.
3. **Issue.** Draft the letter from the maintained templates at `/tools/letters.html`. Templates cite the by-law and rule breached; they never restate or reinterpret it. Compliance periods default to 14 days (7 for a final warning) and may be varied with reason noted in the letter record.
4. **Serve.** Print for hand-delivery or notice-board posting, and email where an address is on file. Record the method of delivery against the letter.
5. **Follow up.** On or shortly after the compliance date, the officer inspects and records the outcome: remedied (close the issue, keep evidence), or not remedied (escalate one rung; the letter record links all escalations to the same issue).
6. **Fees.** Administrative charges are imposed only by *fee-notice* letters, stating the amount, deadline and appeal rights. Charges are set out in the estate's published fee schedule.

## 5. Service standards

- Reports and observations acknowledged within **3 working days**.
- Warning and compliance letters issued within **7 working days** of the breach being verified.
- Compliance follow-up inspection within **5 working days** of the deadline.
- Residents who reported a breach are told the outcome when it closes.

## 6. Records

Every step is filed in the estate records with a reference: issues (KL-IR), visits (KL-SV), letters (KL-LTR), appeals (KL-AP). Letters state that a copy is retained; internal working notes are kept out of the resident-visible history. Records are retained per the estate's retention practice and PDPA obligations.

## 7. Appeals

Every warning, compliance notice and administrative charge letter carries the standard appeal clause: written appeal to the Council care of the Management Office within 14 days, decided per the [Appeals Policy](/policies/appeals-policy/). An appeal does not suspend compliance unless the Council so directs.
