## 1. Purpose

The Enforcement Bylaws (Rules 21–24) let the Management immobilise (clamp) or tow vehicles in defined circumstances and charge for release. This policy says who may clamp, when, and how it is recorded and notified.

## 2. When a vehicle may be immobilised (Rule 21)

Only where the vehicle is:

- (a) indiscriminately or illegally parked, or not authorised to park in the premises;
- (b) de-registered, unlicensed or abandoned; or
- (c) used in breach of the Management Corporation's parking regulations.

Security personnel record the circumstance relied on (a, b or c), the location, date and time, and photograph the vehicle in position **before** clamping.

## 3. Who may clamp

Only trained security personnel (or the contracted towing company) apply and release devices. Devices must be of a type approved by the Management Corporation (Rule 24). Emergency-vehicle access is never blocked by clamping operations.

## 4. Notification and release (Rules 22–23)

1. The office issues a *Vehicle immobilisation / release notice* (letters tool, template **clamp-release**) stating the circumstance, the charges — release S$100; towing/storage at cost plus 50% administrative charge — and the release procedure.
2. Release is arranged at the Management Office during office hours, on production of evidence of ownership; payment is receipted.
3. Where towing/storage applies, actual cost documentation is attached to the record.
4. The Rule 23 disclaimer is reproduced in the notice: no responsibility is accepted for loss, injury or damage however caused to the vehicle, its equipment or contents.

## 5. Records

Every immobilisation forms a record chain: patrol finding or resident report (KL-IR / visit finding) → clamp notice (KL-LTR) → payment/receipt entry. Vehicles clamped three or more times in 12 months are reported to the Council with a view to longer-term measures under the parking bylaws.

## 6. Appeals

Clamp notices carry the standard 14-day written appeal clause; see the [Appeals Policy](/policies/appeals-policy/). Release is not withheld pending appeal once charges are paid or secured.
