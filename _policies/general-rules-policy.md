## 1. Purpose

Rule 1 of the General Rules Bylaws requires all residents to observe and comply with the rules. This policy explains how the estate makes the rules known, and what a resident should do when in doubt about whether something is allowed.

## 2. Making the rules known

- The bylaws are published and versioned on this site (`/bylaws/`) and never change editorially — they are the authoritative text.
- Policies (this layer) say how the bylaws are applied day-to-day; they are improved over time and carry owners and review dates.
- Material changes to house practice are announced via `/announcements/`. Rule 3 reserves the Management's right to amend rules; this site is where the current version always lives.

## 3. Tenants and owners (Rule 2)

Where a unit is leased, the owner is responsible for ensuring tenants receive the bylaws and policies. The office points new tenants to this site at check-in; owners should notify the office of tenancy changes so notices reach the right household.

## 4. If you are unsure whether something is allowed

1. Check the relevant bylaw page first — it links to the policy that applies it.
2. If still unclear, ask the management office **before acting** — especially before any exterior or structural change (see the awnings and renovation policies).
3. **Written approval is only written approval.** Verbal reassurance from a contractor or staff member is not MCST approval.

## 5. When a rule is breached

Breaches are handled under the [Compliance & Enforcement Policy](/policies/compliance-enforcement-policy/) — proportionate, recorded, with appeal rights.
