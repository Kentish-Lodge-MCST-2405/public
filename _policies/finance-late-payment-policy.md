## Policy Overview

This Policy sets out the procedures for implementing the AGM Ordinary Resolution on interest on late payment, the order of deduction of payments, and the recovery (and possible waiver) of legal fees and related costs for MCST Plan No. 2405.

This Policy is **not itself a bylaw**. It explains how the Council, Managing Agent and staff should apply the AGM Ordinary Resolution in day-to-day operations.

---

## 1. Scope and Definitions

- **Scope**
  - Applies to all Subsidiary Proprietors, First Mortgagees and occupiers of lots in MCST Plan No. 2405.
  - Covers all amounts due to MCST: Management Fund, Sinking Fund, Special Levies, late payment interest, legal fees, administrative charges and any other monies.

- **Definitions**
  - **"Due Date"**: The date stated in the contribution notice, demand or invoice.
  - **"Overdue"**: Any amount unpaid **30 days after the Due Date**.
  - **"Interest Rate"**: 15% per annum (or such other rate approved at a General Meeting).
  - **"Council"**: The Management Council of MCST Plan No. 2405.

---

## 2. Interest Calculation and Application

### 2.1 Calculation

- Interest is calculated on a **daily basis** at the approved interest rate.
- Example formula: `Overdue Amount × 15% × (Number of overdue days / 365)`.
- Interest applies from the **31st day after the Due Date** until full payment is received.

### 2.2 System and Records

- Maintain an **interest calculation log** (spreadsheet or system) showing:
  - Due Date;
  - Date interest started;
  - Daily rate and number of days;
  - Total interest added by period.
- All statements to owners should show, clearly separated:
  - Principal outstanding;
  - Interest outstanding;
  - Legal / administrative fees outstanding;
  - Total payable.

### 2.3 Communication with Owners

- All contribution notices should state:
  - The **Due Date**;
  - The **30-day grace period**; and
  - The **interest rate (15% p.a.)** applicable after the grace period.
- When interest starts to accrue, issue a **Late Payment Reminder Notice** indicating:
  - The overdue amount;
  - The date from which interest is applied;
  - The current interest rate; and
  - Contact details for payment arrangements.

---

## 3. Order of Deduction

### 3.1 Application of Payments

- All payments received are applied strictly in this order:
  1) Outstanding **interest**;
  2) Outstanding **legal costs** (including indemnity costs);
  3) Outstanding **Management Fund** and **Sinking Fund** contributions;
  4) All other **levies and monies** due.
- Staff are **not to honour requests** from owners to re-allocate payments in a different order.

### 3.2 Accounting Practice

- Configure accounting / strata management software to apply payments in the above order by default.
- Where manual adjustments are required, document:
  - Date of payment;
  - Amount(s) applied to each category;
  - Staff initials and approving authority.

## 4. Recovery of Legal Fees and Related Costs

### 4.1 When to Consider Legal Action

- Legal action may be considered where:
  - At least **two written reminders** have been issued (including the Late Payment Reminder); and
  - A **Final Demand Letter** has been sent, stating that legal action may be taken and legal costs will be recoverable; and
  - **No satisfactory payment arrangement** has been reached.
- For serious or repeated breaches of by-laws or House Rules, Council may consider legal action earlier if necessary to protect the estate.

### 4.2 Requirement to Write to Council for Approval

- Before instructing solicitors or incurring significant third-party costs (e.g. debt collectors, court filing fees), Management must submit a **written recommendation to Council** that includes:
  - Owner / unit details;
  - Nature and amount of the debt and/or breach;
  - Steps taken so far (reminders, meetings, phone calls);
  - Proposed next step (e.g. letter of demand by solicitors, filing of claim);
  - Estimated costs and basis of recovery (solicitor-and-client or full indemnity);
  - Any known hardship circumstances.
- Councils decision (approve / modify / reject) must be recorded in **Council minutes**.

### 4.3 Written Demand to Owner for Legal Costs

- Once legal and related costs are incurred:
  - Issue a **Written Demand/Notice** itemising:
    - Principal contributions and levies;
    - Late payment interest;
    - Legal fees and disbursements;
    - Administrative charges (if any).
  - Specify that these costs are payable on a **solicitor-and-client / full indemnity basis**, pursuant to the AGM Ordinary Resolution.
  - Attach the statement of account and payment instructions.

### 4.4 Documentation

- Keep copies of:
  - All notices and reminders;
  - Council approval memo for legal action;
  - Solicitors invoices and statements;
  - Any payment plans agreed with the owner.

## 5. Payment Arrangements and Hardship

### 5.1 Instalment Plans

- Council may authorise **reasonable instalment plans** where:
  - The owner demonstrates temporary financial difficulty; and
  - The plan will reasonably clear principal, interest and legal costs within a defined period.
- All instalment plans must be **documented in writing** and signed/acknowledged by the owner.

### 5.2 Temporary Suspension of Further Action

- While the owner is compliant with an agreed instalment plan:
  - Further legal escalation may be held in abeyance; however,
  - Interest continues to accrue unless specifically waived by a **General Meeting resolution**.

## 6. Waiver or Reduction of Interest and Legal Fees

### 6.1 Policy Principle

- As a general policy, **interest and legal fees should not be waived**, to maintain fairness and consistency among Subsidiary Proprietors.
- In **exceptional circumstances** (e.g. genuine hardship, serious medical issues, proven management error), owners may request a **partial or full waiver**.

### 6.2 Submission of Waiver Request

- Owners should submit a **Waiver Request Form** (see Section 7) and supporting documents to Management.
- Management will:
  - Check the form for completeness;
  - Review supporting documents; and
  - Prepare a short **summary and recommendation** to Council.

### 6.3 Council Recommendation

- Council will consider the waiver request having regard to:
  - The reasons and evidence provided;
  - The owners payment history;
  - The impact on other Subsidiary Proprietors; and
  - Any precedent that may be set.
- Council may recommend:
  - Full waiver;
  - Partial waiver; or
  - No waiver.
- Councils recommendation should be recorded in **Council minutes**.

### 6.4 AGM / General Meeting Approval

- Waivers that materially depart from the approved interest and legal fee regime should, where practical, be **endorsed at a General Meeting**.
- The waiver request may be listed as a **specific agenda item** in the AGM Notice, for example:
  - "To consider the request by the Subsidiary Proprietor of Unit #XX-XX for waiver of late payment interest and legal fees".
- The agenda item should be accompanied by a **concise summary of Councils recommendation**, avoiding unnecessary personal details.
- The General Meeting may resolve to:
  - (a) waive interest and/or legal fees in full;
  - (b) waive them in part; or
  - (c) not waive them.

## 8. Process Checklist for Management

- **On late payment**:
  - Issue contribution notice (with Due Date, grace period and interest rate stated).
  - After 30 days: send Late Payment Reminder (interest begins from day 31).
  - If still unpaid: send Final Demand Letter.
  - If still unresolved: prepare Council memo for legal action approval.

- **On legal action**:
  - Obtain Council approval in writing (or via minuted meeting).
  - Instruct solicitors; keep records of all costs.
  - Issue itemised written demand including legal fees.

- **On waiver requests**:
  - Provide Waiver Request Form to owner upon request.
  - Upon receipt, check completeness and supporting documents.
  - Prepare summary and recommendation to Council.
  - If appropriate, include as a specific item on the next AGM agenda for decision.
