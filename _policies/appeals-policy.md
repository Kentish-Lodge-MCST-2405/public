## 1. Purpose

Residents affected by an enforcement decision may ask the Council to review it. This policy states how an appeal is made, considered and decided. It is deliberately a written process: appeals are decided on the record, which keeps them fair, consistent and inexpensive to administer.

## 2. What can be appealed

Any letter that carries an appeal clause:

- first and final written warnings;
- 14-day compliance notices (unauthorised erections/installations, renovation works, general rectification);
- notices of administrative charge;
- vehicle immobilisation / release notices.

## 3. How to appeal

1. **In writing.** Deliver a written appeal to the Management Office for the attention of the Council — by hand, post, or email to the office address on the letter — within **14 days** of the date of the letter.
2. **State the grounds.** The appeal must identify the letter (its KL-LTR reference) and state why the resident says the decision is wrong or should be varied.
3. **The office registers it.** The appeal is recorded in the appeals register with a KL-AP reference, an acknowledgment letter is issued, and the appealed letter's status is marked *appealed*. Registration does not suspend the requirements of the letter unless the Council directs otherwise in writing.

Convenience is not part of the test. Late appeals may be admitted only if the Council is satisfied there was good reason for the delay, and the Council's decision to admit or refuse a late appeal is final.

## 4. Consideration

- The Council considers the appeal **on the written record**: the letter, its linked issue history, the resident's grounds, and any written response the office provides. The Council may invite further written submissions; it is not obliged to hold a hearing.
- Target: decisions within **28 days** of receipt, communicated by the *appeal-outcome* letter template.
- A councillor who issued or is personally involved in the matter does not take part in the decision.

## 5. Outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Upheld** | The letter is set aside as stated in the decision (any charge is withdrawn/refunded). |
| **Varied** | The letter stands as modified — e.g. longer compliance period, reduced charge, approved with conditions. |
| **Dismissed** | The letter is confirmed and remains in full force. |

The decision letter states the effect of the outcome and any conditions. The Council's decision is final within the Management Corporation; it does not affect any right a resident has under the Building Maintenance and Strata Management Act or otherwise at law.

## 6. Records

Appeal files (KL-AP) hold the grounds, all correspondence and the decision, linked to the underlying letter and issue records. They form part of the estate records.
