## 1. Purpose

Rule 16 lists renovation works that require **notification and deposits** to the Management before commencement. This policy sets out how a resident notifies, what the office checks, and what happens when works start without notification.

## 2. Notifiable works (Rule 16 — summary, by-law prevails)

Changing wall/floor tiles; erection or demolition of walls, partitioning, installation of windows and grilles; repositioning of internal doors and walls; false ceilings; built-in cabinets/wardrobes and large fixtures; any works involving sand or cement; installation or alteration of electrical and plumbing works; removal of major items; installation of air-con compressor units on external walls.

Works that will **not** be approved are listed in the Renovation Works That Will Not Be Approved Bylaws — no deposit is taken for these because the answer is no.

## 3. Procedure for residents

1. **Before engaging a contractor**, submit a written renovation application to the office describing the works, contractor details and expected period (see also the Application for Renovation Works and Renovation Period bylaws).
2. Pay the required deposit as advised for the notifiable items.
3. Do not commence until written acknowledgement/approval is received. Contractors must report to security (Contractors to Report to Security Personnel bylaws).
4. On completion, request the post-renovation inspection; deposit refund/reconciliation follows inspection.

## 4. Office handling

1. Log the application against the unit; check the works against Rule 16 and the not-approved list; check structural implications (Building Structure bylaws) and engage specialist advice where structural works are proposed.
2. Issue written acknowledgement/approval with any conditions; record the deposit.
3. Inspect progressively for structural works; inspect at completion before releasing the deposit.
4. Refuse (with reasons, in writing) anything on the not-approved list or that compromises structure or insurance.

## 5. Works without notification

Unnotified works discovered by visit or report are handled with the letters tool template **renov-14**: stop works, then within 14 days either restore or regularise (application + deposit, conditions may include rectification). Continued non-compliance escalates per the [Compliance & Enforcement Policy](/policies/compliance-enforcement-policy/).
